Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Forgeon (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our gaming community platform—including when you follow communities, create threads, build your profile, and participate in reward programs tied to your activity.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile. When you register, we collect information such as username, email address, and password (stored using secure hashing). Your profile may include a display name, avatar, bio, links, and preferences you choose to add.

Community activity. We collect content you post in threads (text, attachments, edits), follows and membership in communities, reactions, reports, and moderation actions that apply to your account.

Rewards and engagement. To operate rewards (for example, based on how much you post or engage in a community), we process metrics such as post counts, timestamps, community identifiers, and eligibility flags. We may use this data to detect abuse, prevent fraud, and improve recommendations.

Technical data. We automatically collect certain device and usage data, such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser type, app version, crash logs, and diagnostic events—similar to most online services.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, secure, and improve the Service (feeds, search, notifications, moderation tools).
  • Show your profile and posts to other users according to visibility settings and community context.
  • Calculate and display rewards, ranks, or badges tied to participation rules.
  • Communicate with you about the Service, policy updates, or support responses.
  • Comply with law, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect users and the platform.

3. Public and community-visible information

Threads and many profile fields are intended to be visible to other members of Forgeon or to the public, depending on product settings. Do not post sensitive personal information you are not comfortable sharing broadly.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage where needed to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure basic performance, and reduce fraud. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may limit certain features.

5. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers who help us host the Service, send email, analyze reliability, or prevent abuse—under contracts that require appropriate protection. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or integrity of Forgeon, our users, or the public.

If communities integrate third-party tools in the future, those tools may collect information under their own policies; we will aim to disclose that clearly where it applies.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Some community content may remain visible if copies exist in caches or backups until refreshed or expired.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can update many profile fields in-product. To exercise other rights, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your request.

8. Children

Forgeon is not directed to children under the age where parental consent is required in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. International users

If you access the Service from outside the country where our servers or vendors operate, your information may be processed in those countries, which may have different data protection rules than your home country.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact legalforgeon@protonmail.com.
